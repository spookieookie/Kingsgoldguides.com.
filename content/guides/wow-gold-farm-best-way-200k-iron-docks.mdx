## Getting to Iron Docks

Iron Docks is in **Gorgrond on Draenor**. Bring a character that can clear Warlords of Draenor dungeon packs quickly, plus plenty of bag space. A vendor mount is handy if you have one.

The video shows the clear through the yards, platforms, bridges, and dock areas. Watch the pulls once before you start copying the route.

## The Clear

1. Enter Iron Docks and clear the first open yard.
2. Pull nearby trash together and use your AoE instead of fighting every mob separately.
3. Move through the platform and cannon areas, staying on the route rather than running into empty side areas.
4. Continue along the docks and clear the larger packs and encounters you reach.
5. Loot the bodies, finish the run, then sort the bags before resetting.

Check the instance rules for your chosen difficulty. If you are resetting quickly, keep the hourly instance limit in mind.

## Sorting the Loot

Keep **Sumptuous Fur** and other materials until you have checked their price. Check BoE equipment before vendoring it, especially weapons and appearances you do not recognize. Sell the gray items so you have space for the next clear.

I would track a few runs together. One lucky item can make one run look much better than the next five.

## About the 200,000 Gold Headline

That is the title of the original video, not a guaranteed total from one dungeon clear. Some of the value can depend on selling materials or equipment. The prices and version shown in an old video may be different from the game you are playing now.

Try a few clears, count what you actually brought back, and decide whether you would rather keep running Iron Docks or move to another farm.
