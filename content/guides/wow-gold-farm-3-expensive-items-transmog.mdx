## The Three Items

These are three transmog items from the original video. I would check each one on the auction house before picking a farm. An expensive listing is a reason to look closer, but you still need a drop and a buyer.

| Item | Location used in the video | What to run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Flatbow | Gundrak, Zul'Drak, Northrend | The dungeon trash route |
| Crested Scepter | Blackfathom Deeps, Ashenvale | The entrance and interior trash packs |
| Blade of Wizardry | Karazhan, Deadwind Pass | The eligible early raid trash |

## Advanced Flatbow

Head to **Gundrak in Zul'Drak** with a character that can clear the packs quickly. Work through the trash, loot as you go, then leave and reset when the instance rules allow it. Check the other weapons and armor too. Do not vendor every green just because the bow did not drop.

## Crested Scepter

The route in the video uses **Blackfathom Deeps in Ashenvale**. Clear the entrance packs and keep going through the caves. Keep the uncommon weapons for a price check before you sell the rest to a vendor.

## Blade of Wizardry

Go to **Karazhan in Deadwind Pass** and follow the early trash clear shown in the video. Karazhan is a raid, so do not assume you can reset it like a normal dungeon. Check the current lockout rules for the version and difficulty you are playing.

## How I Would Sell Them

Check the exact item and appearance, the other listings, and any sale information your addon has. A rare appearance may sit for a while. I would rather understand whether it sells than keep cutting the price every time I check the mailbox.

These are older-content farms. Before spending a night on one item, confirm that it still drops from the content you are running in your version of WoW.
