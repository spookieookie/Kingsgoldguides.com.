## Five Spots to Try

These are the five instant-respawn farms from the video. I would pick one, check what drops, and move if the place is crowded or the items are not worth much on my server.

## 1. Mizjah Ruins, Northern Stranglethorn

Work around the **Mosh'Ogg ogres** and the broken structures. Clear the outside packs, loop through the camp, and come back around as they respawn. Loot the greens and check them before vendoring. This is old-world transmog loot, so do not expect every pack to pay much.

## 2. Gordunni Outpost, Feralas

Start along the outside of the ogre camp, then clear the mobs around the tents and structures. Keep circling rather than running away from the spot after one poor pull. Check the rare-looking drops and current transmog listings before deciding how long to stay.

## 3. The Path of Conquest, Shadowmoon Valley

This is the **Outland** version of Shadowmoon Valley. Follow the path through the **Eclipsion** mobs, pulling groups and looping back through the next packs. Watch for **Netherweave Cloth**, green equipment, and the other Outland drops shown in the video.

## 4. Elemental Plateau, Nagrand

Circle the elemental area and kill the **Raging Fire-Souls** shown in the route. Loot each pull and keep moving between the spawns. Check **Mote of Fire** prices before heading out if materials are the main reason you want to try this one.

## 5. Bash'ir Landing, Blade's Edge Mountains

Loop around the purple structures and the **Bash'ir** mobs. The guide shows a mix of Netherweave, elemental materials, Apexis Shards, and equipment. Sort those separately so you can see which part of the loot is actually worth keeping.

## After a Session

Check the greens before you vendor them, then look at what your materials will sell for. A price tooltip is not a completed sale. You may need to keep listing transmog until the right buyer turns up.

Respawns, drops, and old-world versions have changed over the years. Use the video to find the spots, then test the current version rather than treating an old gold total as today's rate.

For another old-world mob spot, look at the [Southern Barrens Bael Modan route](/guides/wow-gold-farm-southern-barrens-infinite-respawn-200k). The [Burning Steppes cloth farm](/guides/burning-steppes-mageweave-runecloth-farm) focuses on Mageweave and Runecloth from a four-group loop.
