## The Spot

The Goblin Power Shovel route is in **Northern Stranglethorn**, in the **Venture Co. mining area**. Focus on **Venture Co. Miners**. Nearby Geologists are not a confirmed drop source just because they share the area.

Watch the location in the video before you travel. This is a moving route through the mobs, rather than one spot where I would stand waiting.

## How I Run the Loop

1. Move through the mining area and find the next Venture Co. Miner.
2. Land, tag the mobs, kill them, and loot.
3. Continue to the next group along the path.
4. Circle back through the same area as the mobs return.
5. Stop to clear your bags before you run out of space.

A fast character with easy instant attacks makes the movement less annoying. Bring an auction-house addon if you use one, but do not let looting and price checks stop every pull.

## What to Keep

**Goblin Power Shovel** is the target. Keep it for a proper item and price check if it drops. Also check the green weapons and armor before you vendor the rest. Old appearances can be easy to miss when you are clearing bags in a hurry.

## The Price in the Title

The 150,000 gold number comes from the original video. It is not a guaranteed drop value on your server today. Getting the shovel and finding someone who wants to buy it are two different parts of this farm.

I would check the current listings and whether the item still drops from the mobs in the version I am playing before spending hours here. If you need quick, predictable gold, waiting on one rare transmog weapon may not be what you want.
