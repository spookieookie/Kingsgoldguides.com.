I'm at the **Throne of Kil’jaeden in Hellfire Peninsula** farming **Motes of Fire** from **Incandescent Fel Sparks**. Ten motes turn into one **Primal Fire**, and you can compare the two before deciding what to put on the auction house.

The route is simple: run straight back and forth, kill the sparks, and loot as you go. There's no complicated circuit to remember.

## Before you go

Bring a character that can handle the mobs comfortably and make room in your bags. Check **Mote of Fire** and **Primal Fire** prices before heading out.

This walkthrough comes from my **August 15, 2017** video, recorded during Legion. The location and method below follow that recording. Current drops, respawn timing and earnings haven't been retested for this write-up.

In the video I recommend **Bear Tartare** for its movement benefit at the time. Treat that as part of the old setup. Check what your food and movement abilities actually do in your version before spending gold on them.

## Getting to the Throne of Kil’jaeden

Head to **Hellfire Peninsula in Outland** and find the **Throne of Kil’jaeden**. This is the outdoor farming area shown in the video, rather than an instance entrance.

Once you're there, look for **Incandescent Fel Sparks**. Those are the mobs I'm killing for the motes. You can check the target's identity on [Wowhead's Incandescent Fel Spark page](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=22323/incandescent-fel-spark).

## Run straight back and forth

1. Start at one end of the spark area and kill the **Incandescent Fel Sparks** along your path.
2. Loot the bodies before moving on.
3. Continue straight through the area, then turn around and work back the other way.
4. Keep repeating the same path while checking which sparks have respawned.
5. Count your motes after a short test before settling in for a longer session.

I specifically recommend keeping it straight in the recording. Don't zigzag around trying to make the route more complicated than it needs to be.

I'm finding more mobs when I turn back in the original footage. That doesn't establish a fixed respawn timer for today's game, especially with other players farming the same area.

This is an **outdoor farm**. Turning around starts the next pass; you aren't leaving and resetting a dungeon.

## Stick to the sparks

Don't pull everything nearby just because it's in range. In the video I point out another target that keeps hitting me without dying, and warn against aggroing it.

Keep your attacks on the sparks you're farming and be careful with wide AoE around unrelated mobs.

## What we're keeping

**Motes of Fire** are the main drop I'm after. Combine **ten Motes of Fire into one Primal Fire** when the combined material is the better sale.

In my original market, Primal Fire was the option I preferred. That isn't a reason to combine every mote automatically today.

Compare like with like:

- The total price of **ten Motes of Fire**.
- The price of **one Primal Fire**.
- What remains after selling costs, and whether either option is actually moving.

A high listed price doesn't help if the material sits unsold. Keep track of what sells rather than treating the auction value in your bags as gold already earned.

## About the old hourly claim

The original title is **World Of Warcraft LITTLE TO NO EFFORT 15k-30k An Hour**. That's the headline from the 2017 recording, not a verified current hourly return.

In the video I say it took about **a minute and a half to get the first Primal Fire's worth of motes**. That's one short result, not a measured hour. I also discuss Primal Fire prices around **300 gold on my server** and much higher prices elsewhere. Those were historical market observations, not current prices or proof that every listing sold.

The useful part is the simple material route. Do a short run, count what drops, and use your own current prices. If the sparks are heavily contested or the materials don't justify the time, move on.
