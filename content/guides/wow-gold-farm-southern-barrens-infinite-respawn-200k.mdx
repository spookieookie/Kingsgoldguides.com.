## Head to Bael Modan

This is the farm at **Bael Modan Excavation in Southern Barrens**. Stay around the dig site: the pit, tents, ramps, ruins, and the nearby enemy packs are the route.

Bring a character with quick tagging and AoE, plus empty bags. The point is to keep working around the same compact area as the mobs return.

## The Loop

1. Start beside the excavation pit and tag the nearby Bael'dun enemies.
2. Pull them together if your character can handle the group.
3. Kill and loot, then move to the next pack.
4. Work around the tents, ramps, and ruins.
5. Circle back through the middle and repeat as the packs respawn.

If another player is killing the same packs, check whether enough mobs are left to make the loop worthwhile. A fast respawn does not help much if you have nothing to tag.

## What You Are Looking For

Keep the cloth, check the green equipment, and look at the recipes before vendoring anything unusual. **Pattern: Rich Purple Silk Shirt** is one of the valuable recipe drops discussed in the original guide.

This is why I would check the bags properly after a session. The coins alone are not the reason to run the farm.

## Selling the Loot

Price-check the exact recipe and appearances on your server. Recipes and transmog can sit for a while, even when the listed price looks good. Keep that in mind before buying more of the same item or spending the whole day farming it.

The 'up to 200k' title is from the original video. It does not mean every session produces 200,000 gold. Confirm the drops and route still work in your game version, then judge it by your own runs and sales.
