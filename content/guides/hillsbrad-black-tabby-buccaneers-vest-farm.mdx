I'm in **Hillsbrad Foothills, Eastern Kingdoms**, farming gnolls for **Black Tabby Cat** and **Buccaneer’s Vest**. There are other green items to check along the way, too.

The pet is the rare bonus. In the recording I focus on the vest as the more regular target, rather than expecting a cat every session.

## Find the Hillsbrad gnoll field

Use the field shown in the source video. A **Mudsnout Shaman** is visible in the footage, and I'm killing the different gnolls around that area.

This is an outdoor farm. There isn't a dungeon entrance or instance reset involved.

The walkthrough was uploaded on **November 2, 2017**, during Legion. Follow that version context rather than assuming this is a Classic Era route or a current patch test.

## The original Monk setup

In the recording I recommend a **Monk** with **Black Ox Statue** placed at the spot I show. The idea is to bring mobs in and use the nearby passive damage effect to keep killing them.

I describe the respawns as a **force-respawn loop**: enough mobs have to die before replacements appear. Calling it instant doesn't mean every individual mob has a guaranteed zero-second timer.

Check your current abilities, loot credit and respawn behavior before building a long session around that setup. This write-up doesn't establish that the old passive combination still works.

I also recommend mobility because collecting the loot takes time. I'm using a Rogue in the footage, but I specifically say it isn't my preferred class for moving around this farm.

## Keep killing and loot regularly

1. Reach the gnoll field shown in the video.
2. Kill the nearby gnolls and collect their drops.
3. If reproducing the original Monk setup, check where the statue draws enemies and whether your damage reaches them.
4. Watch that new spawns continue and that you receive loot.
5. Collect regularly and clear bag space before you miss drops.

In the video I suggest a timer of about **a minute and a half** between looting. That is the original timing advice, not a guarantee about current corpse despawns.

The title calls this an AFK farm, but you still need to loot. Stay present to collect the drops and respond if the setup stops working.

## What we're keeping

The named targets are:

- **[Cat Carrier (Black Tabby)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=8491/cat-carrier-black-tabby)**, the item that teaches Black Tabby Cat.
- **[Buccaneer’s Vest](https://www.wowhead.com/item=14175/buccaneers-vest)**, a bind-on-equip cloth chest piece.
- Other eligible **green equipment**, priced individually.

Keep an unbound vest for sale if that's your goal; equipping it binds it. Check the carrier separately from an already learned pet when comparing listings.

I discuss selling other greens or disenchanting them. Check the equipment's resale value before turning it into materials, and only disenchant where your character can do so.

## What my lucky hour was worth

The original title is **World Of Warcraft AFK FARM 100,000 GOLD PER HOUR**. In the video I report around **100,000 gold worth of items** in an hour, including a **Black Tabby drop**, a vest and other greens.

I quote the pet at about **60,000 gold** and the vest at about **10,000 gold** on my server. I also say I'm stocking items and waiting to sell them. Those are historical 2017 valuations, not a completed-sales total.

The pet made that hour much better. Its rarity is part of the appeal, but it also means you can't plan on repeating the same result every hour.

Run a short test, count what you collect and check current prices. Track sold items separately from the auction value sitting in your bags.
