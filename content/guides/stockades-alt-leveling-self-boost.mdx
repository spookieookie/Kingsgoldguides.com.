I'm using a **higher-level character to level an alt through dungeons**. The video shows the setup with two WoW windows, then a run through **the Stockades** where I pull the bosses back toward the lower-level character.

The important parts are getting both characters into the group, keeping the alt close enough for experience, and switching characters before the alt shows as disconnected. Here's how I do it in the recording.

## Before you start

You'll need your lower-level character and a higher-level character that can handle the dungeon. I recommend a max-level character in the video. I also mention using a level 85 character for earlier levels, but that's part of the old setup shown here.

Open **two instances of World of Warcraft**. Windowed mode makes it easier to switch between them. I'm using two monitors in the video, but you can follow the setup with one.

This is an older leveling method from my 2017 recording. Login timing, dungeon experience, and entry requirements are things to check in your version before you commit to leveling an alt this way. The written steps explain the recording; they are not a new test of the method.

## Put both characters in the same group

1. Log in on the higher-level character.
2. Open **Group Finder**, go to **Premade Groups**, and create a listing with a name you'll recognize.
3. Switch to the other WoW window and log in on the lower-level character.
4. Find that listing and apply to join.
5. Switch back to the higher-level character and invite the alt.
6. Return to the alt and accept the invitation.

There's quite a bit of logging back and forth in the video. The setup depends on the character you've just left remaining connected briefly while you switch to the other one.

## Choose a dungeon the alt can enter

I'm demonstrating the run in **the Stockades**. I also talk about the **Deadmines** as an earlier Alliance starting point. Pick a dungeon the lower-level character can enter and the higher-level character can clear comfortably.

Before the run, I show resetting the instance when needed. Both characters then need to be inside the same dungeon. I leave the alt near the entrance while the higher-level character goes through the pulls.

The video description links the [longer Alliance and Horde leveling walkthrough I credit for the method](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q0oD3CF3XOk). Use that source for the wider dungeon progression; my demonstration here focuses on the Stockades setup.

## Pull the bosses without killing them yet

On the higher-level character, I use **Bear Tartare** for movement speed while clearing. That's the food used in this recording.

The main thing is to **aggro the bosses and leave them alive while you collect the pull**. I'm working through the Stockades, keeping the bosses on me, and bringing them back toward the entrance.

You can see me kill a normal mob along the way. The instruction to leave things alive is about saving the bosses for the final pull near the alt, not refusing to kill every trash mob.

I also explain the exception: if a boss has to die to open a door or continue through a dungeon, kill it so you can keep moving. Don't assume every dungeon has the same layout as the Stockades.

## Refresh the alt, then finish the pull

Once I've brought the pull back near the beginning of the dungeon, I switch to the lower-level character again. The point is to make the alt show as connected in the group before finishing the kills.

**Keep the alt close enough to receive experience, but away from the mobs.** If they turn onto the lower-level character, that character can die.

I then log back onto the higher-level character and use AoE to kill the gathered bosses and mobs. In the recording, my alt goes from **level 22 to level 23** after the pull.

If the alt isn't getting experience, check the parts the method depends on: whether it still shows as connected, whether it's close enough, and whether that dungeon is suitable for its level. Don't spend a long session repeating a run that isn't giving the experience you expected.

## Move on when the alt outlevels the dungeon

The idea is to repeat the method in a suitable dungeon, then move to another one as the alt outlevels it. I'm not giving a complete current dungeon-by-dungeon route in this video.

I mention **experience buffs and heirloom gear** as ways to help with the setup at the time. Check what those actually do in the version you're playing before building your plan around them.

## Fast levels won't teach you the class

I say this at the start of the video: boosting an alt like this doesn't give you the same practice playing it. If you want to learn the rotation while leveling, take that into account.

My reason for doing it was to get more alts ready and have more characters for different professions. In the video, I describe the method as taking about half the time of my usual leveling. That's the claim from that recording, not a measured current leveling rate.
