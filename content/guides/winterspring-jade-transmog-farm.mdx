I'm in **Winterspring, Kalimdor**, hunting **Jade transmog gear** from **Frostshard Rumblers**. This is a rare-drop farm: the valuable part is the equipment you find and eventually sell.

## Find the Winterspring spot

Follow the mountain and map shown in the video. The targets are the rock elementals, [Frostshard Rumblers](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=48960/frostshard-rumbler).

This walkthrough follows my **October 17, 2017** recording. The group respawn behavior has not been retested for the current game.

## Cover the five farming areas

1. Bring a group to the mountain area shown in the recording.
2. Spread out across the **five areas** I point out.
3. Keep killing the mobs in your area while other players cover theirs.
4. Loot regularly, then return to your position.
5. Check that you're getting new spawns and loot credit before committing to a long session.

In the original method, keeping the mobs dying causes more to spawn. That's the force-respawn behavior I describe, not a fixed timer or a guarantee that the same setup works today.

I recommend a **mobile class** because collecting loot around the mountain takes time. You can attempt it alone, but covering all those positions takes much longer.

## Keep up with the loot

Make room in your bags. In the recording I warn about looting at the two-minute mark; treat that as the old advice and collect regularly rather than relying on a guaranteed current corpse timer.

Watch whether the mobs you kill still give you loot. A busy group isn't useful if you're missing the items.

## What we're keeping

**Jade transmog pieces** are the named target. I also show the broader equipment loot table because the farm isn't limited to one item.

Check each eligible piece before vendoring it. Don't assume every green is valuable or that a high auction listing means someone is buying.

I say in the video that drops are random. You could spend a long session here and get nothing exciting. A good hour doesn't make the next one predictable.
