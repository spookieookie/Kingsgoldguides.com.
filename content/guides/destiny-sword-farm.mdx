I've got another rare weapon farm for you guys. We're heading to **Dreadmaul Hold in Blasted Lands** for **Destiny**, an epic two-handed sword with a pretty unique model.

The mobs we're looking for are **Dreadmaul Ambushers**. The farm is simple: stay around their approach to the hold, kill them, loot, and keep checking the respawns.

## Before you go

Bring a character that can clear the mobs comfortably, turn on auto-loot, and make some room in your bags. An ability that lets you tag mobs quickly is handy when you're repeating the same pulls.

Look up **Destiny** on your auction house before you head out. Check the listings and how much competition you would have if you got one. This is a rare weapon farm, so decide how long you want to spend here before you start.

## Get to the right version of Blasted Lands

Blasted Lands is in the **Eastern Kingdoms**, south of the Swamp of Sorrows. We're farming around **Dreadmaul Hold in the northwest of the zone**.

If you arrive in the Iron Horde version of the area, speak to **Zidormi** near the road connecting Blasted Lands and the Swamp of Sorrows. Ask to see Blasted Lands before the invasion, then head back to the hold.

Check that you can see and attack the **Dreadmaul Ambushers** before settling in. Your character's zone phase matters here.

[The location setup starts at 0:35 in the video.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HU7mPLBRAAo&t=35s) Use the map and the view of the approach to line up with the farming spot.

## Farm the Dreadmaul Ambushers

1. Find the **Dreadmaul Ambushers** on the approach to Dreadmaul Hold.
2. Kill the mobs at the spot shown in the video.
3. Loot the bodies and check for **Destiny**.
4. Watch for the next spawns and repeat the pulls.
5. Check the rest of your loot before clearing out your bags.

I'm repeatedly working the same small area in the recording. Keep the target name in mind instead of just killing everything you see across Blasted Lands.

[The farming section starts at 2:57.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HU7mPLBRAAo&t=177s) You can see how I'm clearing the mobs and returning to the next spawns.

The quick respawns are part of what I'm showing in the original recording. Give the spot a short test in your version of WoW and see how it behaves before committing to a long session.

## What we're keeping

**Destiny** is the main item we're after. It's **bind on equip**, so keep it unbound if you want to sell it. Equipping your drop to try out the model means giving up that copy's auction house sale.

You'll also see **Runecloth** among the loot in the video. Keep the cloth and check any other equipment you pick up before sending it straight to the vendor.

There's no guaranteed number of kills before the sword drops. You can keep clearing the spot and still leave without one.
