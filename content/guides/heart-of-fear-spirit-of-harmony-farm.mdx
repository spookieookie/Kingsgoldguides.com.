I'm heading into **Heart of Fear in Dread Wastes, Pandaria**, for **Spirit of Harmony**. We're farming the trash before the first boss, rather than clearing the raid.

This is the route from my **September 19, 2017** video. Its quick repeat depended on changing the raid difficulty to bring the trash back. I haven't retested that reset in the current game, so check it before planning a long session.

## Before you enter Heart of Fear

Bring a character that can handle the trash and kill a group with AoE. I explain in the recording that a higher-level character makes this easier; you don't need to copy the character I'm using.

Head to the **Heart of Fear raid entrance in Dread Wastes**. Before entering, I right-click my character portrait and set the raid difficulty to **Heroic**. That is the starting setting I call out in the video.

I credit **I Want to Buy Gold** for bringing the spot to my attention. I didn't discover it; I'm showing the run I was doing at the time.

## Pull the trash before the first boss

The first room is on the way to the farm. You can clear those mobs, but the room before the first boss is the part I'm focusing on.

1. Go through the entrance room and continue toward the first boss.
2. Gather the trash packs shown in the middle, on the left, and on the right.
3. **Leave the boss alone.** I also leave the mobs farther back near the boss out of the pull in this demonstration.
4. Bring the selected packs together, AoE them down, and loot.

Watch where your attacks land. This route stops before a boss kill; it isn't a full Heart of Fear clear.

## The difficulty reset shown in the recording

After the pull, I change the raid difficulty and the trash respawns. That's what made the short loop in the original video possible.

I describe repeating it roughly every minute in that recording. That isn't a measured current run time or proof that the same reset still works. Check the actual trash respawn and loot in your game version. If the mobs don't return, the old repeat loop isn't working for you.

## Motes of Harmony and Spirit of Harmony

Keep **Motes of Harmony** as well as any **Spirits of Harmony**. The [Mote of Harmony item tooltip](https://www.wowhead.com/item=89112/mote-of-harmony) explains the conversion: **ten Motes make one Spirit**. Getting Motes doesn't mean you've found the wrong material.

Count them in the same units when checking your return. Ten Motes are one Spirit's worth of material, not ten Spirits. Don't count the Motes and the Spirit you create from them as separate loot.

Check your version before treating them as auction-house stock. The [MoP Classic Spirit of Harmony item record](https://www.wowhead.com/mop-classic/item=76061/spirit-of-harmony) is **bind on pickup**. This 2017 video isn't a MoP Classic resale guide.

## Is it a 150k gold-per-hour farm?

The original title says **World Of Warcraft How to make 150k gold in an hour**. The short demonstration doesn't show an hour of completed sales, so I'm not using that headline as a verified hourly return here.

For a version where you can sell the materials, check what you actually loot, the current auction price, and what buyers pay after fees. If the reset doesn't work or the materials don't sell for enough, move on.
