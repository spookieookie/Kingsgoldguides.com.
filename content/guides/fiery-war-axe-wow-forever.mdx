I've got another rare item for you guys: **[Fiery War Axe in WoW Forever](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=870/fiery-war-axe)**. This thing has an awesome model, and the Forever version has some pretty interesting upgrades.

If you're wondering what to farm for gold when Forever comes out, this is one I'm watching. We're looking at **Green Scalebane in the Swamp of Sorrows** for the Alliance route and **Grimtotem Naturalist in Feralas** for the Horde route.

## What actually got buffed?

First off, the Forever version gets **15 Strength**. The Classic version doesn't have that. It also swings slower and hits harder per swing, while the listed weapon DPS barely changes.

Here are the tooltips I'm comparing:

| Stat or effect | Classic | WoW Forever beta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | None | +15 |
| Weapon damage | 93–141 | 116–175 |
| Weapon speed | 2.90 seconds | 3.60 seconds |
| Listed weapon DPS | 40.3 | 40.4 |
| Initial fire damage | 155–197 | 231–293 |
| Extra fire damage over 6 seconds | 24 | 60 |

The bigger individual weapon hits aren't the whole story. Look at that fire effect.

Average out the initial hit and include the full damage over time, and Classic comes to **200 damage per complete proc**. Forever comes to **322 damage**. That's a **61% increase to the complete fire effect** when all of it lands.

We'd still need gameplay testing to measure the overall damage increase. The tooltip comparison tells us what changed in the fire proc; the listed weapon DPS is almost the same.

## Could Fiery War Axe be worth millions?

I think this could reach a crazy price in Forever, especially if only a few show up on the auction house early on.

The model gives collectors a reason to want it. The extra Strength and stronger fire effect could also attract players looking for a weapon while they're leveling alts. Those are two different reasons somebody might buy it.

I also look at a Retail auction house listing in the recording and the old Wowhead comments about how crazy this weapon's proc used to be. There's already interest in this axe. What I want to watch is how that carries over to Forever.

The million-gold figure is my prediction. Forever will have its own buyers, listings, and gold supply, so I'll be watching what people actually pay once it launches.

## Before you go

Bring a character that can handle the mobs at your chosen spot and make some room in your bags. We're hunting a **rare epic world drop**, so give yourself time and keep checking the loot.

Fiery War Axe is **bind on equip**. Keep it unbound if you're planning to sell it.

## Alliance: Green Scalebane in the Swamp of Sorrows

If you're Alliance, start in **Stormwind** and head to the flight master. Take the griffin over to the **Swamp of Sorrows**, then make your way to the areas highlighted on the map.

The NPC we're looking for is **Green Scalebane**. Work through those spawn areas and check your kills for the axe.

![Green Scalebane spawn locations highlighted in the Swamp of Sorrows.](/images/guides/green-scalebane-swamp-of-sorrows.webp)

Those are the locations I'm showing for the Alliance farm. Keep the mob name in mind when you're working through the area.

## Horde: Grimtotem Naturalist in Feralas

If you're Horde, we're heading to **Feralas**. The NPC I'm looking for here is **Grimtotem Naturalist**.

Use the two highlighted areas on the map to find the spawns, then work through the Naturalists and check your loot.
