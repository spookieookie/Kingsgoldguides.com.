## The Ghostlands Route

This is the short chest and mob loop around **Sungraze Peak and the Amani Catacombs in Ghostlands**. The route is quick, but the expensive items are rare. I would run a few laps and check the loot before deciding how long to stay.

## Before You Head Out

Bring empty bags, a character that can move through the area quickly, and a way to check item prices. Some of the chests shown in the route are locked, so make sure your character can open the ones you are going after.

Watch the map and the movement through the catacombs in the video. It is easier to follow that once than to keep stopping halfway through a run.

## Running the Loop

1. Start around Sungraze Peak and check the chest locations shown in the video.
2. Clear the nearby mobs and loot them as you move.
3. Continue through the Amani Catacombs and check the interior route.
4. Open the chests you can access and keep the BoE greens for a price check.
5. Finish the loop, clear your bags when needed, and check which locations have respawned before repeating.

## What to Keep

Price-check unusual green weapons and armor. The footage and original guide include old transmog pieces such as **Inscribed Leather Boots** alongside cloth, potions, coins, and vendor loot.

The silver and vendor items are easy to count. A green with a large auction-house value is different: it still has to sell.

## About the 600,000 Gold Title

The original video uses that number as its headline. It is not the normal amount of raw gold from a six-minute lap. Rare drops, chest access, and the prices when the video was recorded all affect the result. Check what you actually loot and what buyers on your server will pay.
