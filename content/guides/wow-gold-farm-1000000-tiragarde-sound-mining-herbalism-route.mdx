## Where I Start

This is the Mining and Herbalism loop through Tiragarde Sound from the video. Bring both professions if you can. You are already covering the ground, so you might as well pick up the herbs along the way.

Start south of Boralus around **Kennings Lodge and Bridgeport**. Watch the map in the video before heading out so you know how the loop joins back up.

## The Route

1. Work through the hills around Kennings Lodge. Check rock faces, ledges, and the slopes beside the road for ore.
2. Head toward Bridgeport, picking up nodes around the bridges, shallow water, and nearby hills.
3. Sweep through **Winterdeep Basin**. Keep checking for both herbs and ore as you move between paths.
4. Follow **Old Drust Road** through the lower hills and back toward the start.
5. Keep moving around the loop. Waiting beside one empty node is usually a waste of the route.

The materials to check include **Monelite Ore, Storm Silver Ore, and the BFA herbs** you gather along the way. Empty your bags before starting and bring a character that can get around the zone comfortably.

## What I Check Before Farming

Look up the materials on your auction house first. An old route can still be useful, but the prices from the video are not today's prices. If the ore barely sells for anything, doing the loop faster will not fix that.

After a few laps, compare what you brought back with the time spent. Keep the materials separate from your other bags so you can see what the route actually produced.

## Selling the Materials

Check the current price and existing supply before posting. You do not need to put everything up at once if the market is thin. The million-gold title on the original video is not a promise that a single lap fills your bags with a million gold.
