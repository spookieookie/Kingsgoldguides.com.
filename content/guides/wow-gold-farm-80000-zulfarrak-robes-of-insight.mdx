## Getting to Zul'Farrak

Head to **Tanaris in Kalimdor** and enter Zul'Farrak in the north of the zone. This is the trash-clearing route from the original Robes of Insight video.

Use a character that can kill the packs without slowing down. Empty your bags before you go, because repeated dungeon clears fill them quickly.

## Running the Farm

1. Enter the dungeon and follow the trash route shown in the video.
2. Pull nearby packs together when your character can handle them.
3. Keep moving through the route and loot the mobs you kill.
4. Save Mageweave and any BoE greens, blues, or unusual appearances for a price check.
5. Leave and reset when the instance rules allow it. Watch the hourly instance limit if you are clearing quickly.

Do not spend the whole run waiting for one specific mob. The video is about clearing the eligible trash and getting more chances at the loot.

## What I Would Check

**Robes of Insight** is the item in the title. The other things worth checking are the BoE greens, other rare equipment, and **Mageweave Cloth**. Vendor the obvious trash after checking the items you do not recognize.

## Selling the Drops

Look at the exact robe and the current listings on your server. An old price in a video does not set today's price, and a rare robe can take time to sell. Keep the vendor gold separate from the value of items still sitting on the auction house.

Before repeating this for hours, confirm the item is available from the version of the dungeon you are running. Old-world loot and dungeon versions have changed over WoW's history.
