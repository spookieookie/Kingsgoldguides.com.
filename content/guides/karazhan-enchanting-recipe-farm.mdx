I'm heading to **Karazhan** for **Mongoose, Sunfire and Soulfrost enchanting formulas**. The idea is to learn the recipes, make the enchants, and sell them. I'm also disenchanting spare greens for materials along the way.

This is the **original Burning Crusade raid in Deadwind Pass, Eastern Kingdoms**. I mix up the continent in the recording; use Deadwind Pass, not Outland. Don't confuse this run with the Return to Karazhan dungeon.

## Before you go

Bring an enchanter that can clear the old raid comfortably and make some room in your bags. Check which recipes you already know before heading over.

The video was uploaded on **August 12, 2017**, during Legion. I show a way to level Enchanting to 600 before the farm. That is the profession setup from the recording, rather than a current skill requirement.

Check the recipe requirements for your version of WoW. Training, crafting and selling rules have changed since the original footage.

## The Ley Shatter setup in the recording

Before the raid, I show **Ley Shatter** as part of the old leveling setup. It turns **one Leylight Shard into three Arkhana**.

I'm buying shards, shattering them, and checking whether selling the Arkhana covers the purchase. This needs an initial investment. It only makes sense when the three Arkhana are worth more than the shard and selling costs.

Compare unit prices. A cheap-looking stack can still be expensive per item, and buying a large batch before checking the result can tie up your gold.

That old 1–600 leveling route isn't something I'd copy blindly today. Check that the recipe gives the result you want in your profession system before spending on it.

## Run the original Karazhan raid

1. Head to the original **Karazhan raid entrance in Deadwind Pass**.
2. Run the raid on your enchanter and loot bosses for the formulas.
3. Look for **Enchant Weapon – Mongoose**, **Enchant Weapon – Sunfire** and **Enchant Weapon – Soulfrost**.
4. Finish the run and check your other equipment drops.
5. Learn any new formula you can use, then price its crafted enchant before making a batch.

These are enchanting recipe drops. Learning a recipe gives you a way to craft repeatedly; it doesn't put a finished batch of enchants in your bags.

In the results section, I show the **Mongoose formula** after the run. You can compare it with [Formula: Enchant Weapon – Mongoose on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/item=22559/formula-enchant-weapon-mongoose). Check your character's raid lockout rather than assuming this is a dungeon you can reset over and over.

## What do I actually sell?

**The crafted enchants are the main selling idea.** Learn the formula, buy or gather the materials, and make the saleable enchant where your version supports it. Don't count the formula as though it were a stack of finished enchants you could immediately auction.

I'm interested in the weapon effects: Sunfire, Soulfrost and Mongoose give buyers different looks to choose from. The recording discusses Mongoose as a popular option, but current demand is something to check on your auction house.

There's also the spare equipment. In this run I didn't get the transmog drops I was hoping for, so I disenchant greens and show **Arcane Dust** among the materials.

Check useful BoE gear before disenchanting it. Keep transmog listings separate from materials when adding up the run.

## Check the crafting margin before posting

Add up every material needed for one enchant, then compare that cost with the finished item's auction price. Allow for the auction cut and possible relisting.

Start with a small batch. In the video I warn against flooding your own market: making a pile of enchants is only useful if buyers will take them at a worthwhile price.

Disenchanting materials need the same check. An auction listing is a possible sale, not gold already collected.
