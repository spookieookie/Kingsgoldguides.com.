I've got a cloth farm in **Burning Steppes** for you guys. We're running through four groups of mobs for **Mageweave Cloth** and **Runecloth**, with some greens and blues along the way.

The route is pretty simple. Bring a good AoE ability, clear a group, and keep moving around the loop. I'm using **Fan of Knives on my rogue** in the video.

## Before you go

Burning Steppes is in the **Eastern Kingdoms**, not too far from Stormwind. If you're Alliance, it's a fairly easy trip over. The video shows the farming area and the four groups, including the one tucked behind the hill.

Make some room in your bags before you start. I picked up everything in the video, and the bags fill up fast. A vendor mount is handy for getting rid of the grey items without leaving the spot.

## The four-group route

1. Start at the first group shown in the video and clear it with your AoE.
2. Work **counterclockwise** through the next groups.
3. Clear the fourth group behind the hill, then keep the loop going.
4. Loot as you go and check which packs have come back before making another pass.

In the recording, clearing the packs caused more mobs to respawn. They didn't all come back at once. Usually about half a group was there when I came around again, so I kept moving between the four spots.

There were other people farming here too. If a group is already cleared, check the next one rather than standing there waiting on it.

## Don't leave the loot behind

I show how you can kill on one pass and loot on the next if you're moving quickly. In the video, I warn about bodies disappearing after roughly **a minute and a half**.

I would just loot each pack if your loop is taking longer. Saving a few seconds isn't worth losing the cloth you came for.

## What we're keeping

**Mageweave and Runecloth** are the main things I'm farming here. Check the greens and blues too. Some can be worth putting on the auction house instead of sending straight to the vendor.

Transmog takes forever to sell sometimes. It can take months, which is why I didn't count it toward regular hourly gold in the video. Keep it if it's worth listing, but don't count the auction price as gold you've already made.

I recommended **TradeSkillMaster** in the video because posting a lot of cloth and gear manually gets old fast. An auction addon is optional; the point is to make the selling part easier when you bring back a full set of bags.

## Is it worth running?

The original video was uploaded on **March 11, 2017**. I talked about getting around **300–400 Runecloth and 600 Mageweave in an hour** at the time. Those are the numbers from that recording, so check what the farm is giving you in your version of WoW.

Look up both cloth prices, run the loop for a bit, and see what you bring back. The gold depends on what those materials actually sell for. If the cloth is cheap or the spot is crowded, I would try another farm.
