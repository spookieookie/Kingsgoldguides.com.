I've got a **Volatile Air farm in The Vortex Pinnacle** for you guys. We're heading to **Uldum in Kalimdor**, flying up to the floating dungeon entrance, and clearing mobs for the material.

In the original video, I'm also working on the mount. You can follow that shorter run or keep clearing for materials. Before you settle in for repeated runs, check that the mobs you want actually come back after a reset.

## Before you go

Bring a character that can solo the dungeon comfortably, turn on auto-loot, and make some room in your bags. Some of the mobs interrupt the run with stuns, so being able to kill them quickly helps keep things moving.

Look up **Volatile Air** on the auction house before heading over. Check the price and competition, then see what a short run gives you.

The recording is from **August 5, 2017**. I mention level 85 and Bear Tartare in that video; those are part of the old setup. Use a character suited to your current version of the dungeon rather than treating the old level or food advice as a requirement.

## Get to The Vortex Pinnacle

Head to **Uldum** and fly up to the dungeon's floating entrance. [I show the entrance at 0:39 in the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTRNWmTc11U&t=39s), so use that view to line up with the platform.

Once you're inside, we're clearing the dungeon mobs and looting as we move between platforms.

## Follow the dungeon route

1. Enter the dungeon and start clearing the mobs along the route shown in the video.
2. Loot the kills and keep **Volatile Air**.
3. Use the **slipstreams**, the little cyclones between sections, to travel to the next platform.
4. Keep clearing the next groups, then choose whether you're stopping for the mount route or carrying on through the dungeon.
5. Check the rest of your loot before emptying your bags.

[The route and slipstream demonstration starts at 3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTRNWmTc11U&t=210s). Keep moving between the groups, but don't leave the material drops behind.

## Shorter mount run or longer material clear?

In the recording, I run up to the second boss because I'm also trying for **Reins of the Drake of the North Wind** from **Altairus**. That's the shorter route I use for the results discussed in the video.

If you're interested in the mount too, that explains why I stop there. If you're mainly after materials, I suggest continuing through the dungeon for more kills in the original video.

That's the route choice from the recording, rather than a newly tested comparison of which is faster today. Start with a short test and check what you're actually getting.

## Check the reset before repeating

At the end of the demonstration, I jump from the platform and get returned near the entrance. I then leave the dungeon and reset it.

**Check that your target trash packs repopulate after resetting.** Don't assume killing the second boss and resetting will bring back every pack. A viewer also flagged that issue on the original video, and I haven't retested that repeat route in the current version.

The short runs in the recording took around three minutes, and I hit the instance limit quickly. Plan around the game's instance restrictions instead of assuming you can keep entering without a pause.

## What we're keeping

**Volatile Air** is the main material I'm after. Check the equipment too: in the video, I discuss a few transmog pieces and disenchanting as extra value.

Keep those separate when judging the result. A piece listed for transmog still needs a buyer, and disenchanting value depends on what the materials actually sell for.
