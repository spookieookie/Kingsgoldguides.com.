## Where to Go

**Blackwing Descent** is the Cataclysm raid in **Burning Steppes**. This guide follows the old-raid clear from the video, with trash packs, encounters, cloth, vendor loot, and occasional BoE equipment.

Bring a character that can handle the raid comfortably and enough bag space to loot the run. Check the difficulty and lockout before starting.

## The Route From the Video

1. Enter and clear the opening packs and rooms.
2. Move through **The Broken Hall**, looting the dragonkin, constructs, and other trash you clear.
3. Continue through the encounter areas around **Magmaw and the Omnotron Defense System** as shown in the footage.
4. Work through the available **Vault of Shadowflame** and **Den of the Devourer** areas.
5. Sort the loot when you finish. Only repeat clears when the raid's lockout rules allow it.

This is a raid, so do not plan a session around normal-dungeon reset behavior.

## What to Do With the Loot

| Loot | What I would do |
| --- | --- |
| Raw coins and gray items | Count the coins and vendor the trash |
| Embersilk Cloth and other materials | Check the current auction-house price |
| BoE equipment | Check the item and appearance before vendoring |
| Soulbound equipment | Keep it if you want the appearance; otherwise check vendor value |

## Is It Worth Another Run?

I would separate the gold already in my bags from the items I still need to sell. Old raid transmog can be worth keeping, but a high listed price does not mean it will sell tonight.

The original video shows the farm in its recorded game version. Loot rules, vendor prices, and demand can change, so use a clear or two to check what the route gives you now.
