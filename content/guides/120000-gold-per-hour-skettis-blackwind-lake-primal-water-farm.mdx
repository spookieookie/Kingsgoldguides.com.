## Blackwind Lake

Go to **Skettis at Blackwind Lake in Terokkar Forest, Outland**. The targets in the video are **Skettis Surgers** in the water.

Bring a character that can kill them quickly. Water breathing or useful swimming abilities make the underwater part easier, and empty bags save a trip halfway through the session.

## The Route

1. Travel to Terokkar Forest and make your way to Blackwind Lake.
2. Find the Skettis Surgers in the underwater spawn pockets shown in the video.
3. Kill and loot, then move to the next nearby group.
4. Work around the lake's pockets and return as the mobs respawn.
5. Check your bags after a short run before committing to a longer session.

Do not spend most of the run waiting on one mob if another pocket has targets ready. Watch your air and keep the route comfortable for the character you brought.

## What I Check on the Auction House

The important drops here are **Motes of Water**, which can be combined into **Primal Water**, alongside the other elemental drops shown in the footage. Compare the prices before deciding whether to sell the motes or the combined item.

Look at the amount for sale too. Bringing back a lot of material is only useful if there are buyers for it.

## The 120,000 Gold Claim

That number is in the original video title. It depends on the prices and loot used for that example; it is not a promise that a fresh hour on your server pays the same.

I would time a short test, count the drops, and compare them with the current market. If Primal Water is cheap or the drops have changed in your version, move to something else.
