We're heading to **The Misty Strand on Timeless Isle in Pandaria**. Check the **Jademist Dancer battle pet** when you're looking at the gold side of this farm. **Condensed Jademist is a different item**, and it should not be in your auction total.

## Jademist Dancer versus Condensed Jademist

The names are easy to mix up, so keep the loot straight before pricing a run.

| Loot | What it means for the farm |
| --- | --- |
| Jademist Dancer battle pet | The rare pet to check against current pet listings; verify cageability in your version |
| Condensed Jademist | A bind-on-pickup consumable, not auction-house stock |
| Timeless Coins and Lesser Charms | Character rewards to track separately from item sales |

The [Condensed Jademist item record](https://www.wowhead.com/item=104288/condensed-jademist) lists it as bind on pickup. The [Jademist Dancer pet record](https://www.wowhead.com/mop-classic/npc=73355/jademist-dancer) identifies the pet as cageable. Select the version you play when checking database pages; an item tooltip and a pet listing are not interchangeable.

This corrects the earlier write-up, which treated Condensed Jademist as something to sell. Price-check the pet instead.

## Find The Misty Strand

Go to Timeless Isle and use the northwestern shore around **The Misty Strand** as the area to check. Look for the **Jademist Dancer mobs** in the misty elemental area.

[The island map appears at 1:03 in the original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NBBtTfUi-Ng&t=63s). Use the footage to get oriented before spending time on the route.

## Work through the Jademist Dancers

1. Find the Jademist Dancers around the Misty Strand.
2. Kill the mobs your character can handle and loot the pulls.
3. Work between available targets rather than waiting beside one empty spawn.
4. Check your bags for the pet and keep the other loot separate.
5. Stop after a short test and decide whether the drops and competition justify more time.

Bring empty bags and auto-loot. A rare pet farm can give you a session with no pet, so set a time limit before settling in.

## Check the pet market before farming

Look up **Jademist Dancer** in the pet market and check the competing listings. If you already know the pet, check whether you can cage a copy in your version before planning a sale.

Do not price Condensed Jademist as the pet. Do not add character currencies to an auction-sales total. A pet sitting in your bags or on the auction house is still inventory until someone buys it.

## About the 10,000-gold title

The original video is titled **World Of Warcraft Gold Farm Make 10,000 Gold In 10 Minutes**. That is the old video's headline, not a verified current rate or a promise of a pet every ten minutes.

Check your own drops, time, and completed sales. I would move to another farm if the pet market or the waiting between spawns did not make the session worth it.
