## The Misty Strand

Head to **Timeless Isle in Pandaria** and find **The Misty Strand**. The farm follows the elemental mobs around the mist and geyser area shown in the video.

This is a short route to test. Before heading over, look up **Condensed Jademist** and make sure the current price gives you a reason to farm it.

## Running the Farm

1. Move to the Misty Strand and find the elemental packs from the footage.
2. Kill the mobs your character can handle and loot every pull.
3. Work between the nearby spawn pockets instead of waiting beside one empty spot.
4. Loop back as the mobs return.
5. After a short run, stop and check what you have actually looted.

Empty bags and auto-loot help. You do not need to turn a quick old-content farm into a complicated setup before seeing whether you like it.

## What Drops Matter

The guide focuses on **Condensed Jademist**, with **Timeless Coins and Lesser Charms** also coming from the area. Keep those rewards separate when you count the run. Not every useful reward is something you can turn into an auction-house sale.

## About the Gold Total

The original video is titled '10,000 Gold in 10 Minutes.' That is the context for the guide, not a fixed payout from the island. Check whether the relevant items are tradable in your version, what they sell for, and how much you actually collect.

I would try a short session first. If the drops or demand do not support the price in the video, there is no reason to keep farming just to hit the old headline.
