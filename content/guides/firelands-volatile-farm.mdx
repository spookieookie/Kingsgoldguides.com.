We're going into **Firelands** for **Volatile Air, Volatile Life, and Volatile Water**. Bring a **Potion of Treasure Finding** and clear the trash along the route in the video.

**Do not kill any bosses. Leave every one of them alive.** We're here to farm the trash and repeat the run.

## Before you go

Head to the Firelands raid in **Mount Hyjal**. Bring a character that can clear the trash comfortably, turn on auto-loot, and make some room in your bags.

Check the auction house before buying your potion. Look up **Potion of Treasure Finding** and all three Volatiles. The potion is part of the cost of this farm, so an expensive potion can eat up what you make.

## Use Potion of Treasure Finding

Pop the potion when you're ready to start pulling. It gives eligible kills a chance to drop **Tiny Treasure Chests** as extra loot. Open those chests as you go or when you sort your bags between runs.

Check that the buff is active before you start another clear. There's no reason to burn potion time standing outside the raid or sorting auctions.

## Clear the trash, skip the bosses

1. Enter Firelands and use your Potion of Treasure Finding before the first pull.
2. Follow the trash route in the video. [The route section starts at 2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cgn6GY6Iy4c&t=128s).
3. Pull the normal mobs in groups you can handle, kill them, and loot before moving on.
4. Keep your pulls away from bosses. Watch where you aim your AoE, too.
5. Once you've finished the trash route, head outside, reset the instance, and repeat when the game allows it.

Don't turn the run into a full raid clear. **No boss kills, even on the last run.**

## What we're keeping

The three materials I'm looking for are:

- **Volatile Air**
- **Volatile Life**
- **Volatile Water**

Keep those for the auction house. Open your Tiny Treasure Chests and check the rest of your loot before vendoring it. Count what you actually bring back rather than assuming every run will look the same.

## Is it worth running?

The original video was uploaded on **August 3, 2018**. Do a short test in your version of WoW before committing to a long session. Check that you're getting the drops you came for, then price them with today's auction house numbers.

Add up the materials you sell and subtract the potion cost. If the Volatiles are cheap or the potion costs too much, I would move to another farm. A bag full of materials only pays off when someone buys them.
