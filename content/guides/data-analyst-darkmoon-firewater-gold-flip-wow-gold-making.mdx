## What I Am Doing With Firewater

I check **Darkmoon Firewater** around the Darkmoon Faire because fresh supply can change the price. I look for a sensible buy, hold it, and sell later if the price gives me enough room.

It is easy to get the timing idea and still overpay. The first thing I check is where today's price sits compared with the normal range.

## When I Buy and Sell

1. **Watch the Faire calendar.** Check the dates in your game and region rather than using an old date table.
2. **Compare prices during and after Faire week.** More supply can create a buying opportunity, but the price still needs to make sense.
3. **Decide what I am willing to pay before buying.** I do not want to keep raising that number just because a few cheap listings disappeared.
4. **Hold only what I am comfortable having tied up.** Other players can be buying the same item, and the price does not have to rise on schedule.
5. **Sell when the price works.** Check competing supply, selling costs, and what is actually moving rather than waiting forever for a perfect peak.

## Before You Copy the Buy

Check the current Firewater effect in your game version, its price history, and how much is listed. The video explains the market idea, but you need current numbers before spending your own gold.

I would rather miss a flip than buy an item after it has already run up just because I saw a video about it. The Faire coming around again does not guarantee the same price pattern every month.
