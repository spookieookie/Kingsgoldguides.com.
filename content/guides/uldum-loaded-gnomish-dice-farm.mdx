I've got a rogue farm in **Uldum** for you guys. We're after **[Loaded Gnomish Dice](https://www.wowhead.com/item=63269/loaded-gnomish-dice)**, a toy you can pickpocket and put on the auction house. The greens, loose gold, and lockboxes are a little extra along the way.

Bring a rogue with **Stealth, Sap, and Pick Pocket**. Pickpocket the mobs first, then clear the group and move on.

## Before you go

Uldum is in **Kalimdor**. Head to the raised area shown at the start of the video and begin on the right side. There's a staircase leading down through the farming area; that's the path I'm following.

Make some room in your bags and check the auction house for Loaded Gnomish Dice before you head out. We're farming a rare toy, so the price and competition matter.

I mention using a class-trial rogue in the original video. That advice came from Legion, so check the trial restrictions in your version of WoW before using one. Make sure you can move your loot to a character you will keep.

## Sap the leader and pickpocket the group

1. Stealth and follow the stairs down into the groups shown in the video.
2. Use **Sap on the mob leading the group**. In the recording, that stops the rest of the group from walking away.
3. Work through the mobs and use **Pick Pocket** on each one.
4. Check your loot for **Loaded Gnomish Dice** and keep the lockboxes.
5. Once you've finished pickpocketing the group, clear it with your AoE and loot the bodies.
6. Move to the next group and keep working down the route.

The order matters here. Get the pickpocket loot before you kill the mobs.

## The Pick Pocket macro from the video

This is the targeting macro I put in the original video's description:

```text
#showtooltip Pick Pocket
/cleartarget
/console targetNearestDistance 10.000000
/targetenemy [noharm][dead]
/console targetNearestDistance 14.000000
/cast [harm,nodead] Pick Pocket
```

This is an old macro. If your client ignores its targeting commands, select each mob and use Pick Pocket directly. The macro is optional; you still need to work through the group yourself.

## Keep the dice and open the lockboxes

**Loaded Gnomish Dice** is the main item we're looking for. Keep any greens worth listing too, and check the rest before sending it to the vendor.

You'll also pick up lockboxes. Use your rogue's lockpicking ability to open them. In the video, I recommend opening them for the coins and the chance at extra gear. That adds a little more to the run while you're looking for the toy.

## What I got and how I sold it

In one **30-minute test**, I got **one Loaded Gnomish Dice and seven greens**. TSM showed a regional market value of roughly **21,000 gold for the dice** at the time.

I also talked about getting a dice roughly every 20 minutes in some of my other tests. That was a small sample. This farm is RNG, and I later pointed that out in my own comment on the video. Treat the old **60k-per-hour title** as a projection from those drops and prices, rather than a regular payout.

I had already sold one dice when I made the video, and it took about **10 hours**. Check the listings and undercuts when you post yours. One quick sale doesn't tell you how long the next one will take.

Those results are from the original 2017 recording. Check what the toy sells for now, keep track of your own drops, and see whether the farm is worth your time. The auction value only turns into gold when somebody buys the item.
