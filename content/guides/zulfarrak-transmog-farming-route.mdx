We're going into **Zul'Farrak in Tanaris, Kalimdor**, for **transmog gear**. I'm pulling the trash, freeing the prisoners for extra mobs, and opening the graves before looping back to the entrance.

Bring some patience for the auction house. I say this a few times in the video: these items can take **months to sell**. Run this if you want more transmog to list and you're willing to wait for buyers.

## Before you go

Make some room in your bags and bring a character that can clear the dungeon comfortably. In the recording, a run takes about **six minutes**, depending on your class and how quickly you move through the pulls.

Have the video open for the route. I skip a couple of bosses and pull others along the way, so follow the path I'm showing instead of turning each run into a full boss clear. [The walkthrough starts at 1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmIjvNJY4MI&t=69s).

## Run the Zul'Farrak transmog route

1. Enter the dungeon and pull the groups along the route in the video. Kill and loot the mobs as you work around the instance.
2. Follow the same boss choices shown in the walkthrough. You don't need to stop for every boss.
3. **Leave the witch doctor alive until you've killed the trash following you.** In my recording, killing him too early makes those mobs run back.
4. Free the prisoners from the cage. This brings in extra mobs for you to kill and loot.
5. At the graveyard, **right-click the graves** to bring out more mobs. Clear them and collect the loot before moving on.
6. Continue the **counterclockwise loop back to the entrance**, head outside, reset the dungeon, and repeat when the game allows it.

[Watch the witch doctor section at 1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmIjvNJY4MI&t=92s) if your pulls keep running back. [The graveyard section starts at 2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmIjvNJY4MI&t=122s).

## Check the transmog before vendoring

The gear is the reason I'm doing this run. Check the items you bring back against the auction house before deciding what to list and what to vendor.

Keep track of what actually sells. An expensive listing can sit there for a long time, and reposting the same gear doesn't mean you've earned its estimated value. If you want quick gold to spend straight away, I'd pick another farm.

## What the original loot numbers mean

In the video, **TSM showed 15,132 gold in estimated item value** during the reported **30-minute test**. I also show a vendor comparison of about **40 gold**. That gap is why I keep warning you about treating this as a raw-gold run.

The original video's **30k-per-hour title** comes from projecting auction values. Those items still need buyers, and the prices are from the original recording. They aren't a regular hourly payout.

I would do a few runs, check what dropped, and decide whether I want to keep those items on the auction house. If you've got the patience for transmog and people willing to buy it, this is a route to try.
