I'm in **Wetlands, Eastern Kingdoms**, farming **Wool Cloth** and spare green items. In the original setup I'm using a Monk's **Black Ox Statue** to bring mobs in and **Chi Orbit** to damage enemies around me.

The idea is to stay in one spot and keep looting. I call it an AFK farm in the video, but you still have to collect the drops yourself.

## Find the Wetlands farming spot

Use the **map near the beginning of the video** to find the outdoor fighting area in Wetlands. An **Ebon Slavehunter** is visible in the footage.

This isn't a dungeon run. I'm standing at the spot while mobs come into range, rather than following a long route or resetting an instance.

For a target reference, see [Ebon Slavehunter on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=42043/ebon-slavehunter). Match the area in the recording before setting up.

## The original Monk setup

The video was uploaded on **August 22, 2017**, during Legion. I start with a **level-100 Monk class trial**, return to Stormwind and travel to Wetlands. I recommend Alliance for the trip in the recording because getting there is simpler from that side.

Those are the original travel and character instructions. Don't assume today's class trials have the same access, talents or farming restrictions.

The two abilities I call out are:

- **Summon Black Ox Statue**, which brings nearby enemies toward the statue in the demonstration.
- **Chi Orbit**, which damages enemies around my character in that setup.

I pick passive options for the rest of the build. The footage shows the statue pulling enemies in while the orbiting effect kills nearby mobs.

Check your current abilities before trying to reproduce this. The old talent combination isn't a current build recommendation, and this write-up doesn't claim a new test of it.

## Set up, kill and loot regularly

1. Reach the Wetlands spot shown on the opening map.
2. In the original Monk setup, place the Black Ox Statue where it draws the nearby mobs.
3. Stay positioned so the damage effect reaches enemies around you.
4. Loot regularly and watch your bag space.
5. Check that new kills are still giving you loot before continuing.

In the recording I say the statue stays up for about **15 minutes**. I also recommend looting around **1 minute 45 seconds**, because I describe a **two-minute loot despawn timer**.

Those are timings from the video. Don't treat them as guaranteed current timers or wait until the last second to collect your drops.

Despite the AFK wording, the demonstration still involves regular looting. Stay present to collect the items and respond if the pull stops working.

## What we're keeping

**Wool Cloth** is the main material I name. I show stacks building up, along with some **green equipment** and occasional **patterns**.

Check the equipment before vendoring it. In the video I discuss selling greens on the auction house or disenchanting them for materials. Which option makes sense depends on the item and your character's profession.

Keep cloth value separate from equipment listings. A green item sitting on the auction house isn't the same as a completed cloth sale, and a pattern isn't automatically valuable just because it dropped.

## Check the cloth price before settling in

I quote Wool Cloth at about **one gold each** and describe it selling quickly in my market at the time. That is a historical 2017 price and sale-speed claim, not today's market.

There's no measured current gold-per-hour result here. Start with a short session, count the cloth you actually collect and check the current price. Track what sells after fees before deciding whether to spend more time at the spot.
