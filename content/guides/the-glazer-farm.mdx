I've got another rare item farm for you guys. We're heading to **Mildenhall Meadery in Stormsong Valley** for **The Glazer**, a BoE one-hand mace that looks like a honey dipper.

This thing is freaking adorable. It's the item we're after, and the two mobs I'm focusing on are **Bubbling Mead** and **Flowing Honey**. Get to the meadery, keep clearing those mobs, and loot as you go.

## Getting to Mildenhall Meadery

I'm starting in **Stormwind** in the video. Head into the mage tower's portal rooms, go past the first room, then take the first portal on the right with the **green anchor banner** behind it. [The travel section starts at 0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bqf2dytbJN0&t=37s).

Once you're through, use the flight master and look for **Mildenhall Meadery** in **Stormsong Valley**. If you've got that flight path unlocked, you can let it take you straight over. You don't even have to touch your keyboard for the trip.

That's the Alliance route I show. If you're playing Horde, make your way to Stormsong Valley and head to the same meadery.

## Which mobs drop The Glazer?

The two names you're looking for around the farming spot are:

- **Bubbling Mead**
- **Flowing Honey**

Those are the mobs I'm targeting in the video. Use the map shown in the [farming section at 2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bqf2dytbJN0&t=157s) to find the spot, then start working through the honey mobs.

**The Glazer is bind on equip.** Keep it unbound if you're planning to sell it on the auction house.

## Keep clearing the honey mobs

Make some room in your bags before you start, then keep the farm simple:

1. Find the Bubbling Mead and Flowing Honey around the meadery.
2. Tag and kill them with the ranged or area attacks your class has available.
3. Loot your kills and check for **The Glazer**.
4. Work back through the area and clear the mobs that have respawned.

I'm using **Moonkin Form** here because it makes tagging the mobs easy. If you're on a different class, use your own buttons to keep the kills moving.

I talk about the respawns near the end of the video. Keep checking what's back up and moving between the mobs as they return. The whole idea is to keep getting chances at the mace.

## What about the 191,000–300,000 gold?

That's the range I talk about in the video. This is a **rare transmog farm**, so the gold depends on getting The Glazer to drop and finding an auction house buyer.

Look up **The Glazer on your server** before you commit a few hours. Check the other listings and price yours accordingly. Drop luck and selling time can vary, so count what actually sells.
